### At a Glance
| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|

| **Duration** | 4 semesters (2 years), 120 ECTS; part-time possible |
| **Intakes** | Winter (Oct) AND Summer (Apr) |
| **Teaching Language** | English (100%) |

### Fees
| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Tuition Fee (Non-EU)** | **€0** — No tuition fees for consecutive Master's programmes, regardless of nationality |
| **Semester Contribution** | **€412.30/semester** |

| Component | Amount |

### Application Deadlines (Non-EU)
| Intake | Deadline | Decision By |
|---|---|---|

> ⚠️ Applications received after a deadline are automatically considered for the next semester.

### Application Portal
**https://apply.cs.uni-saarland.de**
(Online system managed by the Department of Computer Science at SIC — NOT uni-assist)

### Academic Requirements
| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Degree Requirement** | B.Sc. in Computer Science, Informatics, or related field; ≥180 ECTS (German equivalent) or equivalent from foreign university |
| **Minimum GPA** | 75% CGPA or 3.00/4.00 GPA **+ top 10% of graduating class** (≈ German grade 2.5 or better; not stated as German grade officially). Meeting minimum does NOT guarantee admission — highly competitive. |
| **ECTS Requirement** | ≥180 ECTS bachelor's; subject knowledge equivalence to Saarland B.Sc. CS in three areas: |

**Required subject knowledge areas:**
1. **Mathematics**: discrete math, real analysis & multivariable calculus, linear algebra, numerical methods, stochastics
2. **Theoretical Informatics**: formal methods, formal logic, computability, complexity (P vs NP)
3. **Practical Informatics**: functional & OOP, data structures & algorithms, databases, software development methods, system architecture

### Language Requirements
| Test | Minimum Score | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **IELTS Academic** | **7.0 / 9.0 overall** | No sectional minimums published |
| **TOEFL iBT** | **95 / 120 overall** | No sectional minimums published |
| TOEFL PBT | 600 / 677 | — |
| Cambridge CAE (C1 Advanced) | Pass | — |
| Cambridge CPE (C2 Proficiency) | Pass | — |
| PTE Academic | 76 | — |

- **Level required**: C1 (CEFR)
- **MOI (Medium of Instruction) letters**: NOT accepted
- **Home editions**: Accepted
- **Expired tests**: Accepted
- **Sectional minimums**: Not published — only overall scores specified
- **German**: Not required

### GRE / GATE Requirement
**MANDATORY for Indian applicants** (from Summer 2026 applications onwards)

> *"Applicants for Computer Science, Cybersecurity, DSAI, Embedded Systems and Visual Computing who have no degree certificate from the European Higher Education Area (EHEA) region, Switzerland, or the UK are required to upload the test result from the GRE General Test or the Indian GATE test."*

- **GRE General Test OR Indian GATE**: both accepted
- **Minimum score**: None — any score accepted
- **Home editions**: Accepted
- **GRE Subject Tests**: Do NOT satisfy this requirement
- **ETS code**: Not specified (upload directly to application portal)

### Entrance Exam / Interview
**None** — admission by document review only (Master's Commission of professors)

### APS Certificate
**MANDATORY for Indian applicants**
- Must submit the **original digitally-signed PDF** from APS India (German Embassy in India)
- Do NOT print, scan, notarize, or stamp — destroys the digital signature
- APS India: https://aps-india.de/

### Uni-assist
**NOT required** — apply directly at https://apply.cs.uni-saarland.de
(A VPD from uni-assist may be submitted optionally if already obtained, but is not required)

### Required Documents
All in **PDF format only**, submitted online — no hard copies, no notarization required:

1. University entrance qualification (school leaving certificate / 12th grade certificate)
2. Copies of 3 most recent degrees (with certified translation if not in English, German, or French)
3. Certified transcript of all courses with grades + grading system explanation
4. CV (including research interests, projects, publications)
5. **Two Letters of Recommendation** (via online review forms in the portal; at least one must be from academia)
6. **APS Certificate** (mandatory for Indian applicants — digitally signed original PDF)
7. **GRE General Test or GATE test result** (mandatory for Indian applicants)
8. **English language certificate** (IELTS / TOEFL / Cambridge / PTE)
9. Additional certificates (optional — CS competitions, MOOCs, etc.)

### Specializations / Tracks
No fixed named tracks — **flexible modular curriculum** (120 ECTS total, including 30 CP Master's thesis). Students tailor coursework from:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Cybersecurity
- Visual Computing (graphics, vision, image processing)
- Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)
- Software Engineering
- Theoretical CS (algorithms, complexity, formal methods)
- Distributed Systems & Networks
- Language & Image Processing
- Cryptography & Geometric Algorithms
- Program Analysis & Formal Verification

**Partner institutes on Saarland Informatics Campus (SIC):** Max Planck Institute for Informatics, CISPA Helmholtz Centre for Information Security, German Research Center for AI (DFKI), and others.

> ⚠️ **IMPORTANT NOTE ON IELTS/TOEFL SCORES**: RWTH Aachen publishes its exact IELTS and TOEFL minimum scores (including sectional minimums) **exclusively in a PDF document** titled *"Sprachanforderungen der RWTH Aachen"* (Language Requirements at RWTH Aachen). This PDF is publicly accessible at:
> **https://www.rwth-aachen.de/global/show_document.asp?id=aaaaaaaaabpixrg**
> The HTML language requirements page does not list specific scores — it only links to this PDF. Applicants must open this PDF directly to read the exact thresholds.

**Known requirements from official SSE and RWTH pages:**
- Only **Academic IELTS** is accepted (not General IELTS) — confirmed by SSE application page
- TOEFL **MyBest scores are NOT accepted** — minimum must be achieved in a single test session
- Only tests whose results can be verified by the test provider are accepted
- **Exemption**: Applicants with school-leaving or academic degree from UK, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, Canada, or USA are exempt from English test requirement

- **German**: NOT required for admission — 4 CP German language course is taken during the program

### GRE Requirement
**MANDATORY for Indian applicants**

Official verbatim from SSE application page:
> *"An applicant must have one Graduate Record Examination (GRE) General Test result that meets all of the following requirements:*
> - *quantitative reasoning score: above 75th percentile (among 25% best)*
> - *verbal reasoning score: above 15th percentile (among 85% best)*
> - *analytical writing score: at least 3.5"*

**Exception** (does NOT apply to Indian applicants):
> *"The GRE is not required for applicants with citizenship of the European Union or European Economic Area or who completed their secondary education in Germany."*

**Submission:**
- GRE results must be submitted **before the application deadline** (March 1, 2026 for WS 2026/27)
- Optional: submit via ETS using institution code **8504** (Aachen U Technology)
- GRE results must be verifiable online

### Entrance Exam / Interview
**None required**

### APS Certificate
**NOT required** for Indian applicants. RWTH Aachen only requires APS for applicants from **China, Vietnam, and Mongolia**. India is not on this list. Confirmed by thorough search of RWTH International Master Application page — zero mentions of APS for India.

### Uni-assist
**NOT required** — applications go directly through RWTHonline. RWTH Aachen processes international master applications in-house through Division 2.1 – International Admissions.

### Required Documents
All submitted online via RWTHonline — no physical documents required:

1. **GRE General Test certificate** (mandatory for Indian applicants; must be submitted before deadline)
2. Bachelor's degree certificate(s)
3. Official transcript of records
4. **Module descriptions document** (maps your courses to the 5 SSE subject areas)
5. **English language certificate** (Academic IELTS or TOEFL iBT — see language requirements PDF)
6. Passport copy

> Note: The exact document checklist is provided in RWTHonline upon application.

### Specializations / Tracks
No named tracks — **flexible elective selection** across 5 subject areas:
1. Theoretical Foundations
2. Communication
3. Data & Information Management
4. Applied Computer Science
5. Software Engineering
