APS Certificate (mandatory for India) 7. GRE General Test or GATE result (mandatory for India) 8. English language certificate (IELTS/TOEFL/Cambridge/PTE) 9. Additional certificates (optional) |

Partner institutes: Max Planck Institute for Informatics, CISPA Helmholtz Centre. |
