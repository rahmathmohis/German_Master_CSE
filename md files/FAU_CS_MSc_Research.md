## 1. Exact Program Name
**Informatik (M.Sc.)** — also referred to as "Computer Science (M.Sc.)" in English contexts

## 2. Faculty
**Faculty of Engineering (Technische Fakultät)**  
Department of Computer Science (Department Informatik)

## 3. Degree Awarded
**Master of Science (M.Sc.)**

## 4. Duration
- **Full-time:** 4 semesters (2 years)
- **Part-time:** 8 semesters (4 years) — also available

## 5. Intakes (Semesters)
**Both Summer Semester (SS) and Winter Semester (WS)**

## 6. Teaching Language
**Primarily German** (some modules taught in English)  
⚠️ This is NOT an English-taught program. German language skills are mandatory.  
The FAU CS department explicitly states: "Informatik at FAU is not an international degree program!"

## 7. Tuition Fee for Non-EU Students (from SS2027 onwards)
**€4,000 per semester** (full-time)  
- €2,000 per semester for part-time study (halved)
- Applies to newly enrolled students and students changing subjects from Summer Semester 2027
- Students already enrolled in WS 2026/27 are exempt
- Students who completed their Bachelor's at FAU or another German university are exempt

**Source:** https://www.fau.eu/studying/international-students/application-and-enrollment-for-international-applicants/tuition-fees-for-students-from-non-eu-states/

## 8. Semester Contribution/Fee
**€82 per semester** (Student Services contribution / Semesterbeitrag)  
This is due regardless of tuition fees, for ALL students.

## 9. Non-EU Application Deadline for Summer 2027
**January 15, 2027**  
(Application portal opens: October 15, 2026)

## 10. Non-EU Application Deadline for Winter 2026/27
**July 15, 2026**  
(Application portal opens: May 2, 2026 — based on general FAU pattern)

The Informatik MSc page explicitly states: "Bewerbungsschluss für das Sommersemester ist der 15. Januar, für das Wintersemester der 15. Juli."

## 11. Application Portal URL
**https://www.campo.fau.de**  
(campo.fau.de — FAU's online application portal)

## 12. Degree Requirement
A **Bachelor's degree (or equivalent Diplom)** in a closely related field is required.

**Specifically accepted as "fachspezifisch" (directly related):**
- Bachelor's or Diplom in Computer Science (Informatik)

**Accepted as "fachverwandt" (closely related):**
1. Bachelor's or Diplom in:
   - Computational Engineering
   - Information and Communication Technology (IKT)
   - Electrical Engineering/Electronics/Information Technology (EEI)
   - Business Informatics (Wirtschaftsinformatik)
   - Mechatronics
   - Medical Engineering with EEI specialization
   - Mathematics with Computer Science minor
   - Technical Mathematics (Technomathematik)
2. Two-subject Bachelor's (B.A.) with Computer Science as first subject
3. First State Examination (Lehramt) with Computer Science as teaching subject

The degree must be from a **public or state-approved university**.

## 13. Minimum GPA in German Grading Scale
**2.50 (= "gut" / good) or better** in the German grading scale (where 1.0 is best, 4.0 is passing)

**Alternative path:** If GPA is below 2.50, applicants may still qualify if they have completed modules from semesters 3–6 of the CS Bachelor's curriculum (or equivalent) worth **60 ECTS** with an average grade of **at least 3.0**.

**Note:** Applicants who don't qualify directly may be invited to an oral entrance examination (mündliche Zugangsprüfung).

## 14. ECTS Requirement
**Minimum 140 ECTS** from completed modules (Faculty of Engineering requirement)

For early application (before completing Bachelor's): Must have completed at least 140 ECTS. The Bachelor's degree must be submitted at the latest within 12 months of starting the Master's program.

## 15. English Language Requirement
The Informatik MSc page states: **"Sufficient knowledge of English language (TOEFL-Test, UNICert II)"**

Based on FAU's orientation guide for language tests (https://www.fau.eu/studying/degree-programs/special-ways-to-study/international-degree-programs/orientation-guide-language-tests/):

| Test | B2 Level (minimum) | C1 Level |
|------|-------------------|----------|
| **TOEFL iBT** | 80 | 95 |
| **TOEFL CBT** | 210 | 230 |
| **TOEFL PBT** | 547 | 570 |
| **IELTS** | 5.5 | 7.0 |
| **UNICert** | Level II | Level III |
| **TOEIC** | 785–944 | 945–990 |
| **Cambridge** | FCE / BEC Vantage | CAE / BEC Higher |

**⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTE:** The Informatik MSc requires "UNICert II" level English, which corresponds to **B2 level (IELTS 5.5 / TOEFL iBT 80)**. However, the FAU orientation guide explicitly states: "Universities may freely determine which language tests are accepted and which minimum score applicants must achieve." The specific IELTS/TOEFL sectional minimums are **NOT published** on the FAU website for this program.

**No sectional minimums are specified** for IELTS or TOEFL for the Informatik MSc. The program only mentions "TOEFL-Test, UNICert II" as acceptable proof of English proficiency.

**Note:** Since the program is primarily German-taught, English proficiency is a secondary requirement. The primary language requirement is German (DSH 2 / TestDaF 4).

## 16. German Language Requirement
**MANDATORY — DSH 2 or TestDaF 4 (in all four parts)**

Accepted certificates:
- **DSH 2** (Deutsche Sprachprüfung für den Hochschulzugang) — Level 2
- **TestDaF 4** — at least level 4 in each of the four parts
- Goethe Certificate C2
- German part of the Feststellungsprüfung (assessment examination)
- Österreichisches Sprachdiplom C2 (ÖSD C2)
- Deutsches Sprachdiplom Level II (DSD II) — at least B2 in all parts
- telc Deutsch C1 Hochschule
- telc Deutsch C2

**⚠️ Without German language proof, admission is NOT possible.**

## 17. GRE Requirement
**Not required.** No GRE requirement is mentioned anywhere on the FAU website for the Informatik MSc.

## 18. Entrance Exam or Interview Requirement
**Possible oral entrance examination (mündliche Zugangsprüfung):**
- Applicants who cannot be directly admitted based on their documents may be invited to an oral entrance examination
- This is NOT a standard requirement — it applies only to borderline cases
- The admission committee (Zugangskommission) decides based on submitted documents first

## 19. APS Certificate Requirement
**YES — APS Certificate IS required for Indian applicants**

From the official FAU page:
> "For applicants with qualifications from India, the APS certificate must be submitted when applying for admission to a degree program (Bachelor's degree, State Examination and Master's degree)..."

**Exceptions (APS NOT required if):**
1. The highest educational qualification originates from a country other than India, China, or Vietnam
2. **The first degree (Bachelor's) is relevant for the Master's application** — BUT: "If applicants gained their Bachelor's degree in India and a Master's degree in another country, an APS certificate is still required. In this case, the university degree relevant for the application is the Bachelor's degree."
3. The applicant has passed a Feststellungsprüfung in Germany
4. The applicant has been awarded a scholarship from Germany or the EU

**For most Indian applicants with an Indian Bachelor's degree: APS IS required.**

**Source:** https://www.fau.eu/studying/international-students/application-and-enrollment-for-international-applicants/applying-for-a-masters-degree-program-as-an-international-student/

## 22. Available Specializations/Tracks

The Informatik MSc has a **highly flexible structure** with 4 "pillars" (Säulen) of specializations:

### Pillar 1: Theoretically-oriented specializations (Theoretisch orientierte Vertiefungsrichtungen)
- Theoretical Computer Science (Theoretische Informatik)
- System Simulation (Systemsimulation)
- Discrete Simulation (Diskrete Simulation)
- Cryptography (Kryptographie)

### Pillar 2: Software-oriented specializations (Software orientierte Vertiefungsrichtungen)
- Programming Systems (Programmiersysteme)
- Database Systems (Datenbanksysteme)
- Artificial Intelligence (Künstliche Intelligenz)
- Software Engineering (OSS)
- Software Engineering (PSWT)

### Pillar 3: Systems-oriented specializations (Systemorientierte Vertiefungsrichtungen)
- Computer Architecture (Rechnerarchitektur)
- Distributed Systems and Operating Systems (Verteilte Systeme und Betriebssysteme)
- Communication Systems (Kommunikationssysteme)
- Hardware-Software Co-Design
- IT Security Infrastructures (IT-Sicherheitsinfrastrukturen)

### Pillar 4: Application-oriented specializations (Anwendungsorientierte Vertiefungsrichtungen)
- Pattern Recognition (Mustererkennung)
- Visual Computing
- Electronics and Information Technology (Elektronik und Informationstechnik)
- Computer Science in Education (Informatik in der Bildung)
- Medical Informatics (Medizinische Informatik)

### Special Track:
- **"Informatik in der Fahrzeugtechnik"** (Computer Science in Automotive Engineering) — a special study focus that appears on the Transcript of Records

### Structure Requirements:
- 60 ECTS from elective modules (Wahlpflichtmodule) from at least 3 of the 4 pillars
- At least 10 ECTS and maximum 30 ECTS per pillar
- At least 2 specializations with at least 15 ECTS each
- 15 ECTS minor subject (Nebenfach)
- 10 ECTS project (Projekt)
- 5 ECTS main seminar (Hauptseminar)
- 30 ECTS Master's thesis
- **Total: 120 ECTS**

## Important Notes for Indian Applicants

1. **German is mandatory** — This is NOT an English-taught program. You MUST have DSH 2 or TestDaF 4.
2. **APS certificate required** — Indian applicants with an Indian Bachelor's degree must submit an APS certificate.
3. **Application fee** — €100 per application (from WS 2026/27 onwards) for non-EU applicants.

5. **No uni-assist** — Apply directly through campo.fau.de.
6. **GPA requirement** — 2.50 or better in German scale (roughly equivalent to 60-65% in Indian grading).
7. **The URL fau.eu/education/master/computer-science/ redirects to the BSc CS/IT Security program** — this appears to be an incorrect URL. The correct MSc page is fau.eu/degree-program/informatik-m-sc/.
