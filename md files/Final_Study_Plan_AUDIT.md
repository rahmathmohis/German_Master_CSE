### 🟡 LOR FORMAT FOR GERMAN TUs

German universities expect LORs that are:
1. **On official letterhead** with institution name, address, phone, email
2. **Signed and stamped** (wet signature preferred; digital accepted by most)
3. **Addressed to:** "Admissions Committee, [University Name]" — NOT generic
4. **Content must include:** Duration of relationship, specific project contributions (name the project), technical skills observed, comparison to peers ("top 10% of students I have supervised"), recommendation strength ("I strongly recommend without reservation")
5. **Length:** 1 page max (German preference for brevity)
6. **Language:** English for English-track programs; German optional but not required
7. **For OVGU specifically:** LOM is max 500 words — do NOT exceed
