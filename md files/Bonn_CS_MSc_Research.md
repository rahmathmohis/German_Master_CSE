## 12. Degree Requirement (Bachelor's Degree Needed)
You must have a **Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a closely related subject** recognized in Germany as at least equivalent to a bachelor's degree.

**Related subjects accepted:** Cybersecurity, Mathematics, Business Informatics, Electrical Engineering, Information Technology, Software Engineering, Mechanical Engineering (with sufficient CS content), Geoinformatics, Digital Linguistics (depending on CS/math content).

**NOT accepted:** Bachelor of Computer Application (BCA) — explicitly rejected.

**Specific content requirements for Computer Science MSc:**

| Area | Minimum ECTS Required |
|------|----------------------|
| Mathematical foundations (logic, discrete structures, linear algebra, analysis, numerics, stochastics, geometry) | **18 ECTS** |
| Algorithm Theory, Complexity Theory, and Formal Languages | **14 ECTS** |
| Programming, Software Technology, and Information Systems | **18 ECTS** |
| Bachelor's thesis (scientific, research-based) | **12 ECTS** |

> **Critical warning for Indian applicants:** The university explicitly states that "more than 99 percent of applicants from Bangladesh, India, Nepal, Pakistan or Afghanistan are rejected because the curricula of their degree programs or their theses do not meet our requirements." The most common rejection reasons are:
> - Insufficient training in Theoretical Computer Science (automata, complexity, algorithms)
> - Bachelor's thesis that is merely a software design project (not a scientific research thesis)
> - Not enough credits in algorithm theory

## 13. Minimum GPA Requirement
**No hard minimum GPA cutoff for eligibility** — however, GPA is a key ranking criterion.

- Only grades **better than 2.5 in the German grading system** (i.e., 2.4 or better, where 1.0 is best) earn points in the selection ranking.
- A certified GPA is not required for eligibility but **improves your ranking**.
- The program uses a competitive selection procedure (Local NC / special selection).

> **Note:** Do NOT convert your foreign grades to German grades in the application form — enter your grades in your university's original grading system.

---

## 14. ECTS Credits Requirement
**120 ECTS total** for the full program:
- 88 ECTS from lectures, labs, and seminars
  - At least 31 ECTS in one chosen specialization track (including ≥1 lab and ≥1 seminar)
  - At least 27 ECTS in other tracks (≥6 ECTS in each of at least 2 other tracks)
  - At most 2 labs and 2 seminars total
- 32 ECTS from Master's thesis + presentation

**For admission (prior bachelor's degree):**
- 18 ECTS in mathematical foundations
- 14 ECTS in algorithm theory/complexity/formal languages
- 18 ECTS in programming/software technology/information systems
- 12 ECTS bachelor's thesis

## 15. English Language Requirement

**Required level:** CEFR C1 or higher

**Accepted proof for applicants who completed education OUTSIDE Europe (including India):**

| Test | Minimum Score |
|------|--------------|
| **IELTS Academic** | **7.0 overall** |
| **TOEFL iBT** (old scoring system, 0–120 scale) | **95** |
| **TOEFL** (new scoring system) | **5.0** *(see note below)* |
| **Cambridge Advanced English (CAE)** or higher | Pass (any grade) |
| 3+ years of secondary/tertiary education in Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, UK, or USA | Accepted |

> **⚠️ Important note on TOEFL "new scoring system" (5.0):** The university's website states "a TOEFL score of at least 5.0 (in the new scoring system) or at least 95 (in the old scoring system)." The "new scoring system" with 5.0 likely refers to the TOEFL iBT Superscoring or a revised reporting format introduced by ETS. The traditional TOEFL iBT (0–120 scale) minimum is **95**. Verify the current TOEFL scoring format with ETS and the university before applying.

> **⚠️ Critical for Indian applicants:** Even if your entire bachelor's degree was taught in English in India, you MUST submit a TOEFL, IELTS, or CAE certificate. A "medium of instruction" letter is NOT accepted.

**No sectional minimums** are specified — only the overall score is mentioned.

**Certificate validity:** Must be valid until the start of the upcoming semester.

## 18. Entrance Exam or Interview Requirement
**No entrance exam or interview** — admission is based on document review and a competitive ranking system.

The selection process involves:
1. Checking group membership (EU/EEA vs. foreign)
2. Verifying deadline compliance
3. Checking formal admission requirements
4. Computing a score based on selection criteria
5. Ranking all eligible applicants

**Selection criteria for Computer Science MSc (with weights):**
- Good GPA for Bachelor's (weight: 1)
- Good grades in advanced modules on algorithms theory or complexity theory (weight: 2)
- Good grades in modules on computer graphics, vision, audio, or sensing (weight: 2)
- Good grades in modules on artificial intelligence (weight: 1)
- Good grades in advanced modules on mathematics (weight: 2)
- Academic manuscripts (thesis, publications) (weight: 1)
- Subject-related experience in teaching (weight: 1)

## 21. Required Documents List

**Mandatory documents (must be submitted by the deadline):**

1. **Documents about citizenship** (if claiming EU/EEA status — not needed for Indian applicants who will be counted as "foreign")
2. **Degree certificates** — all completed Bachelor's and Master's degrees; OR provisional certificate / proof of expected completion date (if not yet finished)
3. **Transcripts of records** — up-to-date transcripts of all degree programs enrolled in
4. **APS Certificate** — required for Indian applicants (Certificate of Authenticity from German Academic Acknowledgement Bureau)
5. **Complementary documents about prior studies:**
   - Diploma supplement explaining grading and credit hour system
   - Syllabus or module handbook describing contents of relevant modules
6. **Curriculum Vitae (CV)** — including:
   - All educational, professional, and relevant activities in reverse chronological order (from now to end of high school)
   - Overview of countries of residence (>6 months) in the past 5 years
7. **Bachelor's thesis** — in original language; if not complete, a detailed proposal or incomplete version is acceptable
8. **Summary of thesis** — if thesis is NOT in English, French, or German, a self-written English summary is required (must include title page, scientific questions, literature discussion)
9. **Proof of English language proficiency** — IELTS Academic ≥7.0, TOEFL ≥95 (old system), Cambridge CAE, or equivalent
10. **Motivation letter** — entered directly in the online form (max 4,000 characters); must explain career path, reason for choosing Bonn, and name 1–2 specific researchers at the institute you'd like to work with

**Optional but helpful documents:**
- Examiner's assessment of Bachelor's thesis
- Academic publications in international conferences/journals
- Certificates or reference letters confirming software engineering work experience (relevant for AI/Cyber Security programs)
- Certificates confirming teaching/tutoring experience
- Documents confirming contributions to open-source projects, hackathons, etc.

**Documents NOT required (and not helpful):**
- Proof of language of instruction in previous degree program
- Letters of recommendation (unless proving employment duration/tasks)

**Language of documents:** English or German preferred. Documents in Afrikaans, Catalan, Danish, Dutch, French, Italian, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, or Swedish are also accepted. All other languages require a certified translation into English, French, or German.

### Track 1: Algorithmics
- Theoretical Computer Science
- Computational Complexity
- Combinatorial Optimization
- Computational Geometry
- Algorithmic Game Theory
- Computational Analytics
- High-Performance Computing

### Track 2: Graphics, Vision, and Audio
- Computer Graphics
- Computer Animation
- Audio Signal Processing
- Image Analysis
- Computer Vision

### Track 3: Information and Communication Management
- Distributed Systems
- IT Security

### Track 4: Intelligent Systems
- Data Science
- Machine Learning
- Artificial Intelligence
- Robotics
- Autonomous Systems
