# German Universities MSc Programs — Research Report for Non-EU Indian Applicants
*Compiled: August 2026 | All data sourced from official university websites and verified subagent research*

---

## ⚠️ IMPORTANT NOTES BEFORE READING
- All deadlines are for **non-EU international applicants who require a visa** (applies to Indian applicants) unless stated otherwise
- German grading scale: **1.0 = best, 5.0 = fail** (4.0 = minimum pass)
- **APS (Akademische Prüfstelle) certificate is mandatory for ALL Indian applicants** to German universities — required for the student visa procedure
- Semester contribution ≠ tuition fee; it covers admin, student services, and public transport ticket (Semesterticket)
- "uni-assist" is a centralized document verification service used by some (not all) German universities
- **€100 non-refundable application processing fee** applies to all non-EU applicants at FAU (from WS 2026/27), max 3 applications per phase
