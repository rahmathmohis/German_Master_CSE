# University of Bremen — Computer Science Master's Program
## Research Report for Non-EU Indian Applicants

**Research Date:** August 2026  
**Sources:** Official University of Bremen website, Admission Regulations (AO-MSc-Informatik-02-26.pdf, dated 25 February 2026), Master's Programs Flyer 2026

## 1. Exact Program Name
**Informatik (M.Sc.)** — officially "Masterstudiengang Informatik"  
English equivalent: Computer Science (Master of Science)

---

## 2. Faculty / Department
**Faculty 03: Mathematics/Computer Science**  
(Fachbereich 03: Mathematik/Informatik)

---

## 3. Degree Awarded
**Master of Science (M.Sc.)**

---

## 4. Duration
**4 semesters** (standard full-time study period = 2 years)  
Part-time study is possible (for family, health, or employment reasons) and will require a correspondingly longer period.

---

## 5. Intake Semesters
**Both Winter Semester AND Summer Semester**  
- Winter Semester begins: 1 October  
- Summer Semester begins: 1 April

---

## 6. Teaching Language — Is It English-Taught?
**NO. The program is taught primarily in GERMAN.**  
- Language of instruction: **German**  
- The Master's Programs Flyer 2026 does NOT list "Informatik (M.Sc.)" in red (red = English-taught programs)  
- Some courses in elective areas may be available in English, but the program is fundamentally German-language  
- This is a critical point for Indian applicants: **German language proficiency is mandatory**

---

## 7. Tuition Fee for Non-EU Students
**€0 (Zero tuition fees)**  
The University of Bremen charges **no tuition fees** for any students, including non-EU/international students. This is Bremen state policy.

**Approximately €425 per semester** (includes semester ticket for public transport in Bremen)  
- Official wording: "Semester fee including semester ticket approx. 425 Euro"  
- This is the only mandatory payment per semester

## 12. Degree Requirement (Bachelor's Degree Needed)
A first professional qualifying university degree (Bachelor's level) in one of the following fields:
- **Informatik** (Computer Science)
- **Medieninformatik** (Media Informatics)
- **Wirtschaftsinformatik** (Business Informatics / Information Systems)
- **Systems Engineering**
- Or a degree with **no substantial differences** in content, scope, and requirements compared to the above

**Minimum total ECTS:** 180 CP (credit points) in the bachelor's degree  
*(Source: §1(1)(a) of Admission Regulations AO-MSc-Informatik-02-26.pdf)*

---

## 13. Minimum GPA Requirement (German Grading Scale)
**No explicit minimum GPA for admission** — the program has **open admission** (zulassungsfrei/keine Zulassungsbeschränkung).

However:
- If the number of applicants exceeds available places, a **ranking system** is applied
- Ranking is based on up to **100 points total**:
  - Up to **60 points**: Overall bachelor's grade (Grade 1.0 = 60 pts; Grade 4.0 = 0 pts)
  - Up to **40 points**: Grade in CS-relevant subjects from prior studies (Grade 1.0 = 40 pts; Grade 4.0 = 9 pts)
- Grades of 4.1–5.0 receive 0 points in the ranking

**Practical implication for Indian applicants:** While there is no hard minimum GPA, competitive applicants typically need a strong academic record. The German grade equivalent of your Indian CGPA will be calculated (using the modified Bavarian formula or anabin database).

---

## 14. ECTS Requirement
**Minimum 130 CP (ECTS) at the time of application** (if bachelor's degree not yet completed)  
- If bachelor's degree is already completed: full 180 CP required  
- Additionally, **minimum 90 CP specifically in Computer Science** areas:
  - At least **40 CP** in core CS (programming fundamentals, software engineering, databases, OS, networks, information security, computer architecture, computer graphics, machine learning, or AI)
  - At least **15 CP** in Mathematics or Theoretical Computer Science
  - At least **35 CP** in further IT-related content (core CS, math, theoretical CS, or IT applications)

*(Source: §1(1)(b) and §1(3) of Admission Regulations)*

---

## 15. English Language Requirement (IELTS/TOEFL)
**NO English language requirement is specified for this program.**  
- The program is German-taught; English proficiency is not listed as a requirement
- The Master's Programs Flyer 2026 lists the language requirement for Informatik (M.Sc.) as: **"German C1 (B2)"** — no English requirement mentioned
- IELTS and TOEFL scores are **not required** for this program

---

## 16. German Language Requirement
**MANDATORY — German C1 level required for enrollment**

Specific requirements:
- **At time of application:** German language proficiency at minimum **B2 level** (CEFR)
- **For enrollment (by 2 weeks after semester start):** German language proficiency at **C1 level** (CEFR)

**Accepted German language certificates (C1 level):**
- **TestDaF** — minimum total of 16 points, with at least 3 points in each of the 4 parts
- **DSH** — minimum DSH-2
- **Feststellungsprüfung** (assessment test at a Studienkolleg)
- **Goethe Certificate C1 or C2**
- **telc "Deutsch C1 Hochschule"**
- **DSD (Deutsches Sprachdiplom der Kultusministerkonferenz)** Level II C1
- **ÖSD (Austrian Language Diploma)** C1 or C2

**Important for Indian applicants:** If your bachelor's degree was taught in German, this can serve as proof of C1 German proficiency. Otherwise, you must obtain one of the above certificates.

*(Source: §1(1)(c) of Admission Regulations; Master's Application page)*

---

## 17. GRE Requirement
**NO GRE requirement.** The University of Bremen does not require GRE scores for any of its Master's programs, including Computer Science.

---

## 18. Entrance Exam or Interview Requirement
**NO entrance exam or aptitude test required.**  
- The program has open admission (no "T" marker in the Master's Flyer, which would indicate a test requirement)
- Admission is based on meeting the prerequisites (degree, ECTS, German language)
- If places are limited, ranking is based on GPA scores (no interview)

---

## 19. APS Certificate Requirement (for Indian Applicants)
**NO — APS certificate is NOT required for Indian applicants.**  
- The APS (Academic Evaluation Centre) certificate is required only for applicants from **China, Vietnam, and Mongolia**
- Indian students are **not** required to obtain an APS certificate
- The University of Bremen's non-EU application page does not mention APS for Indian applicants

---

## 20. uni-assist Requirement
**NO — uni-assist is NOT required for Master's program applications.**  
- uni-assist (Preliminary Review Documentation / VPD) is required only for **undergraduate (Bachelor's) programs** at the University of Bremen
- For **Master's programs**, international students (including non-EU/Indian applicants) apply **directly to the University of Bremen** through the moin portal
- No VPD from uni-assist is needed for Master's applications

*(Source: University of Bremen non-EU applications page)*

---

## 21. Required Documents List

### At Time of Online Application (uploaded to moin portal):
1. **Current overview of grades and academic achievements** (Transcript of Records) — must be an official university document
2. **Graduation certificate** (if already available)
3. **Current curriculum vitae** (CV) in tabular form
4. **Letter of motivation**
5. **German language certificate** (minimum B2 at application; C1 required for enrollment)
6. Proof of academic achievements in specific CS fields (to verify the 90 CP CS requirement)

### Additional Documents That May Be Requested:
- Language certificates (German and/or other)
- Proof of professional experience, internships, or apprenticeships
- Letters of recommendation

### For Enrollment (submitted in paper form after admission):
1. **Acceptance declaration** (Annahmeerklärung)
2. **All proofs of admission prerequisites** (§1 requirements)
3. **Transcript of Records** (official, showing CP/ECTS earned)
4. **Bachelor's degree certificate** (must be submitted no later than 2 weeks after semester start)
5. **German language certificate at C1 level** (by 2 weeks after semester start, or by December 31 for WS / June 30 for SS)

**Translation requirement:** Documents not in German or English must be accompanied by certified German translations from a sworn translator authorized in Germany.

## Important Notes for Indian Applicants

1. **German Language is the #1 Challenge:** This program is taught entirely in German. Indian applicants must achieve TestDaF ≥16 (min. 3 per section) or DSH-2 before enrollment. Start German language preparation early — ideally 2 years before intended start.

2. **No English-Taught CS Master at Bremen:** The standard Informatik M.Sc. is German-taught. If you need an English-taught program, consider other Bremen programs like "Management Information Systems (M.Sc.)" which is English-taught, or look at other German universities.

3. **Direct Application:** No uni-assist, no APS certificate needed. Apply directly via moin.uni-bremen.de.

4. **Grade Conversion:** Your Indian CGPA will be converted to the German grading scale. The university uses the anabin database (www.anabin.de) for this. A 7.5+ CGPA on a 10-point scale typically converts to approximately 2.0–2.5 on the German scale.

5. **Open Admission:** The program currently has open admission, meaning all qualified applicants are accepted. However, this can change annually if demand exceeds capacity.

6. **Student Visa:** You will need a German student visa. Apply at the German Embassy/Consulate in India after receiving your admission letter.
