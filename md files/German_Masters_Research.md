## KEY NOTES FOR INDIAN APPLICANTS

### APS Certificate
- **APS (Akademische Prüfstelle)** is required for Indian applicants at most German universities
- Apply at: **https://www.aps-india.de** (German Embassy in India)
- Processing time: **4–8 weeks** — apply early!
- **Confirmed required**: Stuttgart ✅, Passau ✅, Bamberg ✅, Bonn ✅, TU Clausthal ✅
- **Likely required**: Bielefeld (standard practice for Indian applicants)
- **NOT required**: TU Berlin (VPD from uni-assist required instead), h_da (likely not required)

### Bavarian Universities (Passau, Bamberg)
- Bavaria charges **€3,000/semester tuition** for non-EU students (since WS 2023/24)
- Passau's own website states no tuition fees — **verify directly** as this may be outdated
- Bamberg: €3,000/semester confirmed
- Total cost at Bamberg: ~€3,111–3,130/semester

### Free Tuition Universities
- TU Berlin, h_da, TU Clausthal, Bielefeld, Bonn: **€0 tuition** (only semester contribution)
- Stuttgart: **€1,500/semester** (Baden-Württemberg state fee)

### English vs German Programs
- **Fully English**: Stuttgart, TU Berlin, Bamberg (ISSS), Bielefeld (IIS), Bonn
- **English option available**: Passau (can be studied entirely in English)
- **Primarily German**: h_da (German required)
- **Mixed**: TU Clausthal (verify)

### GPA Requirements (German Scale — 1.0 best, 4.0 pass)
- **Strictest**: Bamberg (**2.0** or better)
- **Moderate**: h_da (**2.5** or better), Bielefeld (**2.7** or better), Passau (**2.7** or best 70%)
- **Open admission**: Stuttgart, TU Berlin, TU Clausthal (no hard GPA cutoff)
- **Competitive selection**: Bonn (no hard cutoff but grades >2.5 earn ranking points; Local NC)

### English Requirements Summary
- **Highest (C1)**: Stuttgart (IELTS 7.0 / TOEFL 95), Bonn (IELTS 7.0 / TOEFL 95)
- **Mid (B2+)**: TU Berlin (IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 87), Bamberg (IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 90)
- **Lower (B2)**: Passau (IELTS 5.5 / TOEFL 87), Bielefeld (IELTS 5.5 / TOEFL 72)
- **TBV**: h_da, TU Clausthal

### Application Routes
- **Direct (no uni-assist)**: Stuttgart (C@MPUS), Bamberg, Bonn
- **uni-assist required**: Passau, TU Berlin (VPD)
- **TBV**: h_da, TU Clausthal, Bielefeld

### ⚠️ Critical Warning — University of Bonn
- **>99% of Indian applicants are rejected** due to insufficient Algorithm Theory credits (need ≥14 ECTS in formal languages, automata, computability, complexity, logic) and/or thesis being a software design project rather than scientific research
- **BCA degree is explicitly NOT accepted**
- **Medium of instruction letter NOT accepted** — IELTS/TOEFL/CAE mandatory
