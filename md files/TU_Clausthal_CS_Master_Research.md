## 7. Tuition Fee for Non-EU Students
**No tuition fees** (€0 per semester)  
Lower Saxony (Niedersachsen), where TU Clausthal is located, does **not** charge tuition fees for regular degree programs, including for non-EU/international students. This applies to all Bachelor's and Master's programs.  
*(Note: Long-term study fees of €500/semester apply only after the student credit (Studienguthaben) is used up — typically after exceeding the standard study period by 4 semesters.)*

## 12. Degree Requirement (Bachelor's Degree Needed)
A **Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a closely related subject** is required, with:
- Standard period of study of **at least 6 semesters**
- Volume of **at least 180 ECTS credit points**
- From a German university OR a university in a Bologna signatory state

**Related subjects accepted:** Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, Business Informatics, Business Mathematics, Industrial/Applied Mathematics

**For applicants from other fields**, minimum subject-specific requirements must be met (see Section 14 below).

---

## 13. Minimum GPA Requirement
**No explicit minimum GPA cutoff** is stated in the admission regulations.  
However, the program uses an **admission procedure** (Zulassungsverfahren) where applicants are ranked by:
- **Bachelor's degree final/average grade** (must account for at least 51% of the selection decision)
- Additional criteria (motivation letter, professional experience, time abroad, etc.)

**Grading scale note:** German grades run from 1.0 (best) to 4.0 (passing). The selection matrix awards:
- Grade 1.0 = 90 points
- Grade 2.0 = 60 points  
- Grade 3.0 = 30 points
- Grade 4.0 = 0 points

For Indian applicants, grades are converted using the anabin database (KMK/ZAB equivalency tables). A typical Indian CGPA of 7.0/10 or above (roughly equivalent to German 2.5 or better) is generally competitive.

---

## 14. ECTS Credits Requirement
**Minimum 180 ECTS** in a Bachelor's program of at least 6 semesters.

For applicants from **non-CS backgrounds**, the following minimum subject-specific credits are required:
- At least **15 CP** in Basics of Computer Science¹
- At least **15 CP** in Computer Science of Systems²
- At least **15 CP** in Mathematics³
- **Total of at least 45 CP** across these three areas combined
- Plus: successful participation in a **seminar** and a **project/professional internship** in computer science

¹ *Basics of CS: programming, programming paradigms, data structures & algorithms, modelling, logic, formal systems, automata theory, formal languages, complexity*  
²*CS of Systems: operating systems, software engineering, databases, computer systems, computer networks/distributed systems, digital technology, security*  
³*Mathematics: analysis, linear algebra, and one of: stochastics, optimisation, or numerics*

**Conditional admission** is possible with up to **30 ECTS** of conditions to be fulfilled during the Master's program.

## 15. English Language Requirement

**Overall minimum: IELTS Academic 6.5 / TOEFL iBT 80**

The CS Master program (6.40.65E) accepts the following as proof of English proficiency:

| Test | Minimum Score |
|------|--------------|
| **IELTS Academic** | **Band 6.5** (overall) |
| **TOEFL iBT** | **80 points** |
| TOEFL PBT | 550 points |
| Cambridge CAE | Minimum grade "B" |
| Cambridge CPE | At least grade "C" |
| UNIcert | At least Level III |
| CEFR | At least C1 level |

**Sectional minimums:** Not explicitly specified in the admission regulations — only overall scores are mentioned.

**Exemptions from English requirement:**
- Native English speakers
- Applicants with a Bachelor's degree from: USA, Great Britain, Canada, Ireland, Malta, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa

**Test validity:** Results must not be older than **2 years** at the time of application.

---

## 16. German Language Requirement

**Generally required: DSH-2 (= TestDaF TDN 4, approximately B2/C1 level)**

Since the program is taught in German/English, German language proficiency is generally required. However:

**Exemption from German requirement:** Applicants whose native language is English OR who have excellent English language skills (proven by an accepted English test — see Section 15) are **exempt** from the German language requirement.

**For Indian applicants:** Since English is not the native language, you would typically need to provide either:
1. Proof of German language skills (DSH-2 or TestDaF TDN 4), OR
2. Proof of excellent English skills (IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 80+) to be exempt from the German requirement

**Accepted German language certificates:**
- DSH-2 or DSH-3
- TestDaF (TDN 4)
- Goethe-Zertifikat C1/C2
- DSD I/II
- TELC B2/C1
- ÖSD-Zertifikat
- IB German

## 17. GRE Requirement
**No** — GRE is **not required** for this program.

## 18. Entrance Exam or Interview Requirement
**Possibly** — The program uses an **admissions procedure** (Zulassungsverfahren).

- A **subject-specific aptitude test** (Eignungsfeststellungstest) may be conducted if conditional admission would require more than 11 ECTS of conditions
- The aptitude test verifies foundational CS knowledge
- Applicants are invited in writing by the entrance and admissions review committee
- For straightforward applications meeting all requirements, no interview/test is typically required

---

## 19. APS Certificate Requirement for Indian Applicants
**YES — APS certificate is REQUIRED for Indian applicants**

From the official IZC checklist (dated 11.12.2025):
> *"APS certificate only if your educational qualification is from a country subject to APS: China, India and Vietnam"*

**APS (Akademische Prüfstelle)** is the Academic Evaluation Centre that verifies Indian educational documents for German university applications.

**Processing fee with APS certificate:** €100 (reduced from €125 for non-APS countries)

From the official IZC Application Checklist (dated 11.12.2025):

**General documents (all applicants):**
1. Secondary school leaving certificates and transcripts
2. University transcripts for all previous semesters (if previously studied)
3. **Bachelor's degree certificate and complete list of grades for all years of study**
4. Official translations (German or English) of all application documents
5. **APS certificate** (mandatory for Indian applicants)

7. Complete and up-to-date **curriculum vitae (CV)**
8. **Proof of payment of the processing fee** (€100 for Indian applicants with APS)
9. Valid **passport/identity card** (if available)

**Additionally, if applicable:**
- Proof of previous study periods in Germany (enrollment/deregistration certificate, transcript, certificate of good conduct)

**Upload instructions:**
- All documents as clearly legible **PDF files** (all pages complete)
- Photos of documents are NOT accepted
- Mobile phone photos with shadows/skewed angles are not accepted

**For enrollment (after admission):**
- Original Bachelor's degree certificate and list of grades
- Passport (original)
- Personal appearance at IZC required (enrollment cannot be done remotely)

## 22. Available Specializations / Tracks

The program does **not** have formally named specializations, but students can build a profile through elective courses. The curriculum includes compulsory elective areas:

1. **Secure and Cooperative Information Systems**
2. **Visual and Interactive Computing** (Computer Graphics, HCI)
3. **Artificial Intelligence**
4. **Software Systems Engineering**
5. **Data Science and Big Data Technologies**
6. **Application subjects** (e.g., Geomatics, Energy Management, Information Technology, Economics, Operations Research, Sport+Technology, Computational Engineering)
7. **Theoretical and Methodological Foundations**

**Special Track:**
- **Research Track** — For particularly qualified and research-interested students; includes a one-semester research project as preparation for possible doctoral studies

**Program structure:**
- Semesters 1–3: Advanced CS courses + theoretical/methodological foundations + application area courses
- Semester 4: **Master's thesis** (can be written in cooperation with a company)
