## 12. Degree Requirement (Bachelor's Degree Needed)
1. A **Bachelor of Science degree** (B.Sc. Informatik at TU Darmstadt is the reference program), or an equivalent degree
2. The degree must provide competences amounting to **at least 180 ECTS credit points** (or equivalent workload)
3. Of these, **at least 60 ECTS** must not differ significantly from the core courses of the B.Sc. Informatik program at TU Darmstadt (in content, academic level, and depth)

> The Academic Eligibility Checklist (PDF) is available for self-assessment to compare your previous coursework with the core courses. Partial overlaps are counted proportionally.

**Relevant fields:** Computer Science or closely related subjects (e.g., Information Technology, Software Engineering, etc.)

---

## 13. Minimum GPA Requirement
**No GPA/grade requirement** — Grades and GPA are explicitly **NOT used in admission decisions**.

> From the official page: *"Grades / GPA: Are not used in admission decisions; only course content is assessed. Professional experience: Admission decisions are based exclusively on academic coursework."*

The admission decision is based **solely on the content and coverage** of your coursework (whether you have at least 60 ECTS equivalent to the core B.Sc. Informatik courses), not on grades.

---

## 14. ECTS Requirement
**Minimum 60 ECTS** in subjects that match the core courses of the B.Sc. Informatik at TU Darmstadt

The total degree must be at least **180 ECTS** (or equivalent workload).

The 60 ECTS must cover the following core competency areas:
- **Theoretical Computer Science:** Automata, Formal Languages and Decidability; Modeling, Specification and Semantics; Propositional and Predicate Logic
- **Applied Computer Science:** Algorithms, data structures, programming
- **Technical Computer Science:** Computer architecture, hardware
- **Software Engineering:** Software development methods
- **Mathematics:** Linear algebra, analysis, discrete mathematics, probability/statistics

| Section | Minimum Score |
|---------|--------------|

| Quantitative Reasoning | ≥ 165 |

- Winter semester: **August 20**

## 18. Entrance Exam / Interview Requirement

### Is there an entrance exam?
**YES — a written Entrance Exam exists**, but it is **conditional**, not mandatory for all applicants.

### When is it required?
The Entrance Exam is required **only when** the required entrance competencies **cannot be verified** from the submitted application documents alone.

### Entrance Exam Key Facts:
| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| Format | Computer-based (in Moodle), on pre-configured university computers |
| Duration | **90 minutes** |
| Language | English |
| Venue | **On campus at TU Darmstadt ONLY** (no remote/off-site sittings) |
| Fee | None (free) |
| Winter 2026/27 date | **September 1, 2026 at 14:00 CEST** |
| Summer semester | Typically first week of March |
| Scope | Core courses of B.Sc. Informatik (same exam for all English-taught Master's programs) |

### Can GRE substitute for the Entrance Exam?
**YES** — Submitting valid GRE scores (meeting all three thresholds above) **qualifies applicants for exemption** from the Entrance Exam.

**GATE** (Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering) with an overall score of **≥ 750** is also accepted as an alternative exemption route.

### Important Notes:
- No rescheduling is possible
- Absence = non-participation (cannot retake within the same cycle)
- If you miss the exam, you must reapply in a future semester OR submit qualifying GRE/GATE scores
- No interview is required

## 19. APS Certificate Requirement for Indian Applicants

**YES — APS certificate is MANDATORY for Indian applicants.**

> From the official TU Darmstadt website: *"All applicants who wish to apply for a degree program at a German university with a Chinese, Vietnamese or **Indian** school or university degree [need an APS certificate]. This applies to both bachelor's and master's degree programs."*

### What is APS?
The **Academic Evaluation Center (Akademische Prüfstelle, APS)** is a service institution of the cultural departments of the German embassies in cooperation with DAAD. It verifies the authenticity of academic documents and confirms that academic achievements are sufficient for study at a German university.

### Who needs it?
All applicants applying with an **Indian school or university degree** — for both Bachelor's and Master's programs.

### Exception:
- Applicants who have passed the Feststellungsprüfung after a Studienkolleg preparatory course do NOT need an APS certificate.
- Applicants applying for a **doctoral program** do NOT need an APS certificate.

> The APS certificate must be obtained from the German Embassy in India before applying. It is listed as a required document in the application.

## 22. Available Specializations / Tracks

The M.Sc. Computer Science program offers **three specializations**:

### 1. 🔬 Data Science & Engineering
Students cover the entire spectrum from theoretical foundations of data science to implementation and engineering of data-centric systems.

**Course areas:**
- Foundations of Data Science
- Data Science Engineering & Systems
- Data Science Applications

> **Note:** This specialization differs fundamentally from the M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. It focuses on machine learning for data-driven systems and software engineering — NOT on core AI areas like computer vision, NLP, planning, or robotics.

### 2. 🌐 Distributed Computing (formerly "Distributed Systems")
Aimed at students who want in-depth training in the design and programming of distributed/parallel systems, as well as the theory and practice of underlying communication and coordination mechanisms.

**Course areas:**
- Computer Networks and Distributed Systems
- Data-Intensive Systems and Heterogeneous Hardware
- System Modeling and Engineering

> **Note:** The official current name is **"Distributed Computing"** (not "Distributed Systems"). The program page also refers to it as "Complex Networked Systems" in some places.

### 3. 🎨 Visual Computing
Covers the entire spectrum of visual data processing — computer graphics, computer vision, and related areas (digital libraries, machine learning in Visual Computing, VR/AR, biometrics, geometry processing, information visualization).

**Course areas:**
- Computer Graphics
- Computer Vision & Machine Learning
- Integrated Methods of Vision & Graphics

## 23. Program Structure

The program is divided into:
1. **Core courses:** Software & Hardware (practical, technical, and applied CS) + Theory (theoretical CS)
2. **Specialization:** One of the three tracks above (includes seminars, labs, and practical labs)
3. **General education:** Interdisciplinary courses from all TU Darmstadt departments
4. **Master's thesis**

**Total:** 120 ECTS over 4 semesters
